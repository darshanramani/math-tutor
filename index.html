GET TO KNOW YOUR TUTOR

Hi, I’m Darshan.

I believe every student can make progress in math with the right explanation, a little patience, and a space where questions are always welcome.

For the past 6 years, I’ve helped students work through challenging concepts step by step. I focus on the why behind each answer, not just memorizing formulas, so learning feels more manageable and confidence grows naturally.

I hold an M.Sc. in Computer Science from Lakehead University and a B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering. I teach in English, Hindi, and Gujarati.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Where do sessions take place?

All tutoring is in person, either at your home or a local library in Toronto or the GTA. I’ll confirm the location with you before booking.

How long is a session?

Most families choose a 60-minute session, but we can discuss what works best for your child’s attention span and goals.

What does the free first session include?

It’s an introductory, no-cost session to meet your child, understand their goals, and explore the concepts they’d like help with. We’ll then discuss a personalized plan.

Do you help with homework and tests?

Yes. Lessons can include homework support, class-test review, EQAO preparation, filling learning gaps, or challenging students who want to get ahead.

How do I pay?

We’ll confirm the hourly rate and a convenient payment method together before your first paid session.

Can sessions be rescheduled?

Yes. I offer flexible scheduling; please let me know as early as possible so we can arrange another suitable time.
